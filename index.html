About me

Rooted in the Himalayas,
focused on the world.

Growing up in Nepal, one of the world's most biodiverse and climate-vulnerable countries, showed me early on how closely people's wellbeing depends on healthy ecosystems. That experience led me to Environmental Science, and I now hold a Master of Science in the field.

My work brings together rigorous scientific methods, field research, and clear communication. I'm motivated by questions that matter: how to protect natural resources, how communities can adapt to a changing climate, and how evidence can guide better policy and development decisions.

I enjoy working with international organizations, research institutions, NGOs, and mission-driven teams that want to make a measurable, lasting difference for people and the planet.
